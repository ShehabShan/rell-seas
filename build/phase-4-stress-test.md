## Recommendation

**I recommend Concept 4 — The Long Watch, because it is the only concept that survives the actual launch conditions: an unreleased game, one operator on 10–15-minute check-ins, and five members on day one, while keeping the date/milestone honesty that trust depends on.**

One-line reason: waiting is the pre-launch product, and only the Watch makes waiting feel like belonging instead of emptiness, without needing live referees, crew liquidity, or game facts that do not exist yet.

Why this wins despite scoring 2 on top-volume needs: the top-volume systems/rivalry cards (INS-055/059/060/067/069) all describe post-launch play that must stay rumor-tagged until launch (G4/G10, AGENTS.md rule 5), so a pre-launch server cannot resolve them directly — it can only host them honestly, which the Watch does minimally while nailing the needs that are resolvable now (N2 date discipline, N13 containment-as-identity per conflict C7 demand-wins, N10 return doors tied to real milestones per C5 milestones-as-breaker, N11-lite milestone board).

## Runner-up and why it lost

**Runner-up: Concept 2 — The Crucible, because it best answers what players talk about most (six of the top ten) but loses on the constraints that kill small servers.**

Why it lost in one line: its refereed arena needs a crowd to argue and a referee to cool threads, which is the heaviest human load at the exact moment staffing is thinnest and facts are thinnest (rivalry spikes + speculation-policing on every fruit/build claim).

What would have to change for the Crucible to win: either the game launches (real systems facts replace rumor-tagging, G4 resolved) or a second trusted referee appears (load stops depending on one operator's check-ins) — neither holds at launch, so it waits as the post-launch evolution, not the day-one default. Harbor (22) loses on empty-docks day-one plus G6 re-marking load despite the best social infrastructure for later; Ledger House (23) loses on coldest day-one plus heaviest misread-as-official risk despite the best trust apparatus for later.
