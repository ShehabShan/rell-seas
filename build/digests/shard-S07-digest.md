## Shard summary
S07 is the social-organization shard: who players will be together, not just what they will play. Theme one is crew infrastructure — finder, language-tagged matchmaking, pre-launch registry, and war events to house recruiting that already runs ahead of release. Theme two is identity lanes beyond the pirate/marine binary — Marine-story and civilian paths, rank ladders, and professions-as-onboarding with rumor-tag discipline on unverified perks. Theme three is the solo-and-private tension — solo-viable paths and anti-mega norms alongside RP islands and a loud private-server policy so the game reads social-first without gating lone wolves.
