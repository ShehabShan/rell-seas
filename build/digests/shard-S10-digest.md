## Shard summary
S10 is the trust-and-governance shard: a community keeping score on promises and demanding plainer talk. Theme one is the trust ledger — yearly slip accounting, tripwire threats, vague-posting as the core charge, and multilingual pleas for production honesty answered with ledgers, doctrine, and sourced formats. Theme two is accountability infrastructure — auditor voices, hype-discipline norms, leak-literacy tags, and title audits that route suspicion into evidence rather than slogans. Theme three is moderation legitimacy — conduct rules for toxicity and domination gloating, a public multilingual mod charter with appeals, funding-transparency answers to milking charges, and containment rooms for doom, hope, and boycott talk.
