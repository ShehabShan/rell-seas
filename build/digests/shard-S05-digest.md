## Shard summary
S05 is the systems-and-identity shard: rarity done right, bounty-based progression, and a living ocean with NPC fear and reputation. Theme one is earned power — rare-but-fair fruits with pity, bounty-as-strength, and skill-over-fruit combat culture. Theme two is identity over meta — Conqueror fantasy with etiquette, build-by-love libraries, race guides, and showcase indexes. Theme three is structured expertise — progression-feedback, mechanics deep-dives, balance-watch, and rumor-tagged roadmaps that turn theorycraft and anxiety into recruitable expert channels.
