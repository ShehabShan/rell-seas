## Shard summary
S08 is the naval-fantasy shard: ships, sea, and what lives in it. Theme one is ships as home and weapon — customization showcases, moving-ship combat credibility, specific wishlist items, plus submarines as their own interactable-interiors fandom. Theme two is a living ocean — diving/fishing/exploration over fast travel, side quests and race homelands that make the world inhabited, and sea beasts as the naval endgame. Theme three is challenge and structure — aggressive-boss tuning narrated live, spectacle-vs-teacher difficulty split, clan wars and rewarding sea events, and player-written quest concepts harvested with credit under promise-archive discipline.
