## 1. Categories and channels (7 categories, 14 member-visible channels — lean cap observed)

Default: 7 categories / 14 channels at launch, because fewer rooms feel warm at 5 members and every extra room is moderation surface for one operator. All channels English-only except the locked translation blurb.

### A. LIGHTHOUSE ENTRY (read-only except introductions live in Hearth)
- `#read-first-rules` — single source of truth: fan-run disclaimer line + 6 rules + report path + Solo Banner parity line + English-only + generic partner line. One-line purpose: read this first or nothing else makes sense.
- `#welcome-in-pt-fr-es` (read-only, locked) — human-checked static PT/FR/ES blurb only: fan-run notice + English-only scope + generic partner-hub direction + report path. One-line purpose: non-EN arrivals feel seen without opening a moderation surface.

### B. WATCH DECK (containment-as-identity; all under slowmode 60s from day one)
- `#watch-deck` — grief-humor, year-memes, chants, begging-humor as affectionate release; heckler-fatigue rule protects wounded members, never the chant (N13: INS-003/008/105/106/107/108/112). One-line purpose: salt has a room so serious rooms stay serious.
- `#lantern-room` — earnest waiting, hope, hype self-discipline, patience talk; doom and hope both roomed, never mixed into one pile-on (N13: INS-115/121/126). One-line purpose: hope has a room too so doom doesn't own the server.

### C. MILESTONE BOARD (shipped-only; Ledger labeling borrowed; strictest scope discipline)
- `#milestone-board` (Keeper-post-only; members discuss in threads) — shipped milestones only with source link + date-posted + confirmed-vs-rumor label; never words-as-milestones; dateless drops honestly labeled (N2/N11-lite: INS-001/004/012/014/021/112/116/118). One-line purpose: the only place a "fact" lives, and only with a receipt.
- `#receipts-and-rumors` — members post claims/leaks/wishlists here first with mandatory confirmed-or-rumor + source-or-no-source framing; unsourced restatements moved or rumor-tagged, never deleted for respectful criticism (N11: INS-023/118/125; N12: INS-054/128). One-line purpose: detective energy gets a desk instead of a rumor mill.

### D. HEARTH (belonging; return doors tied to real milestones only)
- `#introductions` — who you are, where you sailed from (Shindo continuation never replacement), playstyle in one line (N10: INS-002/073/079/143/144/146). One-line purpose: first voice, lowest stakes.
- `#tenure-and-returns` — tenure honors (day-zero/2019/both-Shinobi-Lives with author credit, no invented lineage), reunion boards for lost-friend groups, low-pressure return doors posted only alongside real milestones (N10: INS-091/143/145/146/002). One-line purpose: leaving and returning are both honorable.
- `#finder` (forum channel with 7-day thread auto-archive) — simple crew/friend finder by playstyle/faction/intent + Solo Banner opt-in flair; listings self-expire unless author re-confirms so no human chases stale posts (N9: INS-081/082/090/091/092). One-line purpose: find your crew before you set sail, or sail bannered solo with equal standing.

### E. OCEAN & HELP (joy freely, scope claims restrained per C4)
- `#ocean-joy` — ships-as-home, naval feedback, ocean/beast enthusiasm showcased; every showcase tagged confirmed-or-community-wish, no spec sheets stated as fact (N8: INS-057/093/094/097/101; C4). One-line purpose: love for the sea, honestly labeled.
- `#help-desk` (forum channel; each question its own thread) — testing-access, platform, scam-check questions; answers sourced-only with benchmarks/specs/parity marked uncertain; device-experience volunteers recognized (N1/N4/N12: INS-015–019/030–041/013/047/049). One-line purpose: one question, one thread, one checkable answer.

### F. COMMUNITY CRAFT (receipt-gated rituals only per INS-118)
- `#patient-craft` — patience manifestos curated as revival triage (never as milestones), myth-writing, slang glossary, with author credit; new entries only with substance/sources attached (N13: INS-110/111/113; C6). One-line purpose: waiting made into craft, not noise.
- `#creator-mirror` — prompt public mirroring of sourced creator material in English with credit frames (no exclusive hoarding); creator corrections welcomed; single-video phenomena routed here not stickied (N14-creator: INS-028/109/152/028-vs-152). One-line purpose: creators as kin, mirrored fast and credited.

### G. SAFETY (one visible channel; everything else private)
- `#scam-watch-and-report` (Keeper-post-only + thread replies) — genuine-link notices, lookalike/fake-game warnings, and the single report/appeals path pinned at top; boycott/doom criticism met with evidence not deletion (N12: INS-013/047/049/123/128; C8 split). One-line purpose: know what's real and where to ask for help.
- Private `#mod-action-log` (staff-only, not member-visible) — every AutoMod hit, referee/evidence-foul call, and Keeper action recorded; no public summary at launch per owner directive. One-line purpose: the non-negotiable private record.

Borrowings placed explicitly: evidence-over-insults pinned in `#receipts-and-rumors` topic + `#ocean-joy` comparison overflow rule (Crucible); Solo Banner parity line in `#read-first-rules` + opt-in flair role + self-expiring `#finder` forum hygiene (Harbor); confirmed-vs-rumor + source-or-no-source labels enforced on `#milestone-board` and `#receipts-and-rumors` (Ledger).

## 2. Role ladder (5 rungs + 1 optional flair + bots)

Default: tenure-plus-steadiness ladder, because purchasable or headcount status would read as pay-to-win and mega-crew officers must earn standing through conduct not headcount (INS-122/146).
- `Castaway` (new) — can read entry + introductions only until onboarding + verification gate passes. For: raid buffer.
- `Watchkeeper` (verified participant in good standing) — can post in Watch/Hearth/Ocean/Help/Craft + open finder/help threads + react. For: the default citizen.
- `Solo Banner` (optional self-assign flair, equal standing with crewed; can hold alongside Watchkeeper/Beacon) — can post LFS as solo without crew pressure; finder filters include solo-welcome tag. For: Harbor parity borrowed structurally (INS-092), not verbally.
- `Beacon` (earned: steady, kind, evidence-honest presence during salt peaks; helpers/device-experience sharers recognized) — can create `#patient-craft` showcase threads + flag evidence fouls via report path + post in `#creator-mirror` without pre-queue. For: proof-of-steadiness prestige.
- `Elder` (earned: long tenure + sustained Beacon steadiness; rare, never purchasable, no lineage invented) — can co-curate tenure honors + myth features with author credit. For: visible tenure-as-identity (INS-146).
- `Keeper` (operator + rare deputies only) — can post in `#milestone-board` + `#scam-watch-and-report`, manage AutoMod queue, grant Beacon/Elder, record private log. For: one-operator control.
- Bots (`AutoMod` system + one helper bot, see §3) — can slowmode-enforce, auto-archive, welcome-DM. For: automation carries the load.

No paid roles, no crew-captain powers, no referee staff at launch (evidence-foul flags are best-effort async queue cleared on check-ins; threads cool and wait if Keeper away).

## 3. Bots and automation (ONE helper bot + native Discord; favor automation over human-online)

Default: Discord native (AutoMod + onboarding + verification + forum auto-archive) + one helper bot on its free plan (default: Carl-bot, because its free welcome-DM + reaction-role for Solo Banner + autorole cover the borrowed needs in one dashboard a novice can learn). One-line reason: one dashboard the owner can learn beats three best-in-class bots nobody maintains. Phase 6 must confirm each helper-bot feature on the free plan; anything unconfirmed falls back to native-only + Keeper batch-handling on check-ins — no custom bot at launch.
- AutoMod (native) — handles: slurs/hate (block + flag), spam/mention-flood, flagged scam-link patterns (N12: INS-013/047/049). Best-effort keyword flag only (false-positive-prone, reviewed on check-ins): "free Robux/EAC giveaway" bait and unsourced "EAC price / release date" assertions, which Keeper reframes to `#receipts-and-rumors` with a rumor label rather than auto-deleting respectful criticism (N1/N2: INS-015/016/001/007; N12: INS-054/128).
- Verification level: Medium (must verify email before sending messages) at open; raise to High if raid/bait spike — preset decision so no deliberation under load. Verification level gates chatting only and never assigns roles by itself.
- Role assignment (feasible mechanism): helper-bot autorole grants Watchkeeper on onboarding completion (free-tier, Phase 6 to confirm); fallback is Keeper batch-grant on 10–15-min check-ins. `<1-day accounts stay Castaway (read-only) until 24h pass or Keeper clears. Server stays healthy either way because reading never stalls.
- Welcome flow: onboarding screen (fan-run + rules + English-only + report path must-accept) → lands as Castaway → helper-bot welcome-DM (fan-run line + #read-first-rules link + Solo Banner opt-in + how to open a help/finder thread). No human welcome party needed.
- Finder hygiene: `#finder` forum native auto-archive after 7 days inactive (no bot needed); helper-bot expiry nudge is best-effort only, otherwise threads simply archive and authors re-post to re-confirm; no human chasing stale posts.
- Help hygiene: `#help-desk` forum native auto-archive after 7 days; wrong answers corrected with sources via Keeper edit/flag on check-ins, not live patrol.
- Milestone hygiene: `#milestone-board` Keeper-only posts; member submissions via `#receipts-and-rumors` using the pinned label template (Confirmed/Rumor + source link or "no source"); helper-bot prompt for the template is best-effort only, enforcement is the Keeper label pass on check-ins.
- Watch hygiene: `#watch-deck` + `#lantern-room` permanent slowmode 60s + AutoMod; no live referee — evidence-over-insults norm is a pinned rule + report reason, cleared best-effort on check-ins.

## 4. Onboarding / verification flow (first five minutes, no human needed)

- 0:00 — Land on onboarding screen: fan-run disclaimer ("FAN-RUN — not official, no official assets") + 6 rules summary + English-only + report path; must accept to enter. Lands as Castaway.
- 0:45 — Read `#read-first-rules` (persistent copy of same) + see `#welcome-in-pt-fr-es` if needed (human-checked blurb; generic partner-hub direction, no links to specific hubs).
- 1:30 — Helper-bot DM: welcome + Solo Banner opt-in reaction + "open your first thread in #help-desk or #finder" guide.
- 2:30 — Email verification (Medium level) unlocks sending messages (native gate, assigns no role by itself); Watchkeeper role arrives via helper-bot autorole on onboarding completion, or via Keeper batch-grant on the next 10–15-min check-in. <1-day accounts stay read-only Castaway until cleared.
- 3:30 — Post in `#introductions` (template: callsign / sailed-from / solo-or-looking / one ocean joy) to get first reaction and find the Watch deck.
- 5:00 — Done: can lurk milestone board, vent in Watch deck, or ask in help. No quiz, no manual approval queue that stalls when Keeper is away.

## 5. Launch-week content plan (7 days, all async, no crowd required)

Default: one Keeper-seeded post per day + zero live events, because a live event with 5 members feels emptier than a slow thread that waits. Each seed honors receipts (INS-118) and ties returns to real milestones only (C5).
- Day 1 — Keeper posts `#read-first-rules` + `#milestone-board` seed (3–5 shipped-only entries with sources, e.g., still-unreleased status + what EAC/CC labels mean with unknowns marked) + `#introductions` self-intro. Goal: trust baseline (N1/N2/N11).
- Day 2 — `#watch-deck` icebreaker: "how long have you been waiting? year-meme welcome" (grief-humor contained here; N13 INS-003/105/108). Goal: first salt, safely roomed.
- Day 3 — `#ocean-joy`: "show your ship-as-home dream, tagged wish-or-confirmed" (N8 INS-093/097). Goal: joy without scope promises.
- Day 4 — `#receipts-and-rumors`: "post one rumor you've heard + source-or-no-source label" demo of evidence-over-insults (borrowed Crucible norm, INS-070). Goal: teach labeling by doing.
- Day 5 — `#finder` + Solo Banner: Keeper posts example LFS + example Solo Banner post; reunion thread for lost-friend groups (N9/N10 INS-090/091/092). Goal: prove solos equal.
- Day 6 — `#patient-craft`: first myth/slang seed with author credit + `#creator-mirror` first sourced mirror with credit (INS-110/113/152). Goal: craft prestige without receipts-cynicism.
- Day 7 — `#tenure-and-returns` + `#lantern-room`: tenure shout-outs (no invented lineage) + "what milestone would bring you back?" (returns tied to real milestones only, C5). Goal: doors open, nothing faked. Keeper posts week-2 note: no fixed schedule owed.

Success at 5 members = 5 intros + 1 rumor labeled + 1 finder post, not 500 messages.

## 6. Weekly routine (sized to 10–15-minute check-ins, not moderator shifts)

Default: 3–4 async check-ins per day when possible + one 30-min weekly batch, because automation + slowmode + self-expiring threads keep the server healthy while curation simply lags when away (G8).
- Each 10-min check-in (phone-friendly): 1) report queue + AutoMod flags (2 min) → 2) `#receipts-and-rumors` label pass: rumor-tag or move unsourced claims (3 min) → 3) `#watch-deck`/`#lantern-room` cooling scan: evidence-foul nudge or slowmode hold, never live debate (2 min) → 4) `#help-desk`/`#finder` solved/archive nudge (2 min) → 5) private `#mod-action-log` one-line entries (1 min).
- Weekly 30-min batch (pick any quiet evening): milestone-board review (anything newly shipped with source? if none, post nothing — never fake to fill), one creator-mirror if sourced material exists (prompt mirroring per INS-028 or skip), one tenure/craft feature with author credit, scam-watch link check.
- Monthly 15-min: G7 blurb still accurate? partner language still generic-appropriate? AutoMod false-positive review. No public mod summary to write (owner directive).

## 7. Fan-run disclaimer — exact placement and copy

Default copy (short, because long disclaimers get skipped): "FAN-RUN — This is a fan-run community, not official. Not affiliated with the RELL Seas developers. No official assets used here. Treat everything as community discussion unless a Keeper post links a primary source."
- Placement (all four, non-negotiable): (1) server description/short-about (first line); (2) onboarding screen must-accept item #1; (3) `#read-first-rules` pinned line 1 + channel topic; (4) alongside-claim line auto-appended to every `#milestone-board` post + `#scam-watch-and-report` notices ("fan curation — check the linked source"). One-line reason: persistent at entry + alongside anything that could be mistaken for official word is what trust needs N1/N2/N11 depend on.

## 8. Safety baseline (automation carries the load; no custom age-verification)

- AutoMod: presets ON (profanity/slurs, spam/mention-flood, scam-link patterns) + custom keyword list (slur variants incl. mod-training cases per INS-062/077, lookalike-link domains, "free Robux/EAC giveaway" bait per INS-013/047); action = block + flag to private log, never public shaming.
- Verification: Medium at launch (email-verified), High on raid signal; <1-day accounts held as Castaway; onboarding must-accept before any post.
- Slowmode: 60s on `#watch-deck` + `#lantern-room` from day one; 30s on `#receipts-and-rumors` during spikes (preset toggle).
- Report flow (single async path, pinned in `#read-first-rules` + `#scam-watch-and-report`): open a private thread in `#scam-watch-and-report` via the helper-bot ticket command (e.g., `/report`, Phase 6 to confirm free-tier command name) OR DM the Keeper directly — both land in an async queue Keeper clears on 10–15-min check-ins, no one needs to be online. Outcome logged privately; appeals via the same path with second-look promise (readable rules with appeals per INS-123). Scam-help: genuine-link list in `#scam-watch-and-report`; victims directed to official support + evidence-preservation steps, never asked for passwords/tokens.
- Mod-action log: private `#mod-action-log` only; no public summary at launch (owner directive) — keeps one-operator load sustainable and avoids trial-by-thread.
- Age-verification: explicitly NOT needed at launch. General game-discussion content with AutoMod + verification level + report flow is sufficient; no age-gated spaces ship. Flag to owner only if a future concept adds age-entangled access (e.g., gated giveaways) — none in this blueprint. (Per AGENTS.md: flag, don't build.)

## 9. What we left out and why (lean-launch doctrine made real)

- Deep ledger archive beyond milestone-board + receipts desk — left out because an empty archive feels dead at 5 and is the largest misread-as-official surface; add only when sourced-answer volume overflows the desk (Ledger deferred, N11-lite suffices).
- Refereed arena / staged bouts / Referee role — left out because bouts need crowds + cooling calls, the heaviest human load when staffing is thinnest; evidence-over-insults norm is kept without the staging (Crucible engine waits for post-launch or a second referee).
- Crew registry / charters / caps / clan-war structures — left out because chartering + domination-policing (INS-122 mega-crew fear) needs liquidity that doesn't exist pre-launch; reunion boards + expiring finder cover belonging now (Harbor institutions wait).
- Build school / trade apparatus / pity/odds tables — left out because every number is an unreleased number (AGENTS.md rule 5, G4/G10); fair-trade norms + identity-build threads suffice until launch replaces rumor-tags with facts.
- Non-EN discussion spaces / ongoing non-EN moderation — left out per G7 owner directive; static human-checked blurb + generic partner direction is the launch maximum (N14 explicitly out of scope at launch). Explicit override noted: sentiment research suggests PT/FR hubs with native-speaker mods, but a solo operator on 10–15-min check-ins cannot sustain that, so English-only + locked blurb stands until staffing changes.
- Custom bot / paid bot tiers — left out; off-the-shelf free bots + native Discord + manual Keeper fallback only, because a custom build is a second project the owner did not approve.
- Public mod/referee summary — left out per owner directive; private log only keeps load sustainable and avoids performative moderation.
- Fixed-schedule rituals / live launch events / giveaways — left out per INS-118 (no ritual without receipts) and C5 (only shipped things reset the ledger); one async seed per day in launch week, then no schedule owed.
- Platform-spec / price / date promises — never shipped; unknowns marked (G1–G6/G10).
