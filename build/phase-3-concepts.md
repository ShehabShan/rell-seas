## Shared ground — applies to all four concepts (not a concept itself)

These are AGENTS.md constraints plus the Gap G7 resolution the owner supplied for this session. They are stated once so the concepts don't relitigate them.

- **Free, never pay-to-win.** No paywalls, no paid roles, no cosmetic framing that reads as pay-to-win. Players demand the oath be kept and spend routed to cosmetics-only approval (N3: INS-042/045/052); the server restates only sourced dev quotes on price/monetization and labels everything else unknown or speculation. Status elsewhere comes only from tenure, recognized help, and good conduct (INS-146/041/065/039) — never spend.
- **Fan-run disclaimer visible by design.** Every concept carries a persistent fan-run notice at entry and alongside anything that could be mistaken for official word (no reuse of official game assets). Trust needs N1/N2/N11 depend on members never mistaking fan curation for dev statements.
- **One operator, 10–15-minute check-ins.** Every concept is sized so automation carries the load by default. Discord terms used once here: AutoMod (Discord's built-in filter that auto-blocks slurs, spam, and flagged phrases before a human sees them); verification level (a server setting that makes new or very new accounts wait before they can chat, which blunts raids); onboarding screen (the pop-up checklist new members see before they get access — used here to surface the fan-run notice + rules before anything else); forum channel (a channel type where each question is its own thread, so help doesn't drown in chat scroll); slowmode (a per-channel cooldown between messages, which cools floods without a moderator present). Every concept includes: entry gating + verification level, AutoMod on, one clear report path, and a private mod-action log as the non-negotiable record. Human-curated elements (refereeing in Concept 2, chartering/listing-freshness in Concept 3) are designed as best-effort async queues: the server stays healthy on automation + 10–15-minute check-ins alone, and curation simply lags when the operator is away rather than breaking anything (G8). Any public-facing mod/referee summary beyond the private log is a concept-level option flagged for the owner in Phase 4, not a launch requirement.
- **No custom age-verification.** None of the concepts gate on age. Where access facts are age-entangled (G2), the server states only what is sourced and marks the rest unknown.
- **No invented facts, dates, or numbers.** All concepts design around G1–G6/G10 by marking uncertainty: no release windows manufactured (N2), no EAC/CC price or access facts stated until sourced (N1/G2), no platform specs or parity promises (N4/G3), no money/odds/pity numbers (N3/N6/G4, AGENTS.md rule 5), no production/IP lore as fact (N11/G5), no private-server or timed-drop rules stated as fact (N9/G6), no boss-tuning numbers (G10). Wishlist and folk-model content stays explicitly rumor-tagged.
- **Gap G7 — RESOLVED for all concepts (owner directive this session).** Launch is English-only. Every concept implements the same minimum non-alienating touchpoint and nothing more: (a) a short static welcome blurb translated once into PT/FR/ES shown at entry, (b) partner-not-poach language that names existing non-EN community hubs as the right home for non-EN discussion and directs speakers there rather than recruiting them away, (c) no non-EN discussion spaces and no ongoing non-EN moderation at launch. Native-idiom understanding (N14: INS-129–INS-139) is therefore explicitly out of scope at launch in every concept; creator symbiosis (INS-109/152/028) is handled in English only. Concepts differ on everything else, not on G7. Flagged as owner decisions for Phase 5 (launch stays English-only either way): whether the partner language names/links specific hubs or stays generic, and whether the static blurb requires human-checked translation.

## Concept 1 — The Ledger House

**Core identity hook.** "Every claim has a source or it doesn't stand." A quiet, evidence-first archive where the server's pride is never being caught inventing a fact.

**Who this is for.** Players burned by receipt-keeping, false alarms, and monetized-access confusion who want one place where date talk, testing talk, and money talk are checkable (N1/N2/N3/N11). Welcomes detective energy (INS-023/125) by giving it a sourced record to work with instead of a rumor mill.

**Category skeleton (6, high level, no channels designed here).**
1. Entry + fan-run notice, rules, report path (onboarding screen surfaces all three before access).
2. Trust ledger (sourced record only: what is confirmed, what is rumor, what slipped — with sources and uncertainty labels; read-first, tightly moderated).
3. Discussion (general game talk; claims challenged to cite or move to rumor framing).
4. Help desk (forum-type Q&A: testing-access, platform, scam-check questions; wrong answers corrected with sources).
5. Community hearth (low-volume introductions, showcases, patience-positive space; salt redirected elsewhere per C7).
6. Containment + moderation transparency (one salt/vent space with slowmode from day one; private mod-action log; scam-watch notices).

**Role ladder (high level).** Newcomer → Verified member → Fact-hand (earned by consistently sourced answers) → Archivist (earned by sustained ledger-quality contributions) → Keeper (operator + rare deputies only). No crew, build, or tenure ladders at launch — sourcing is the only climb.

**What makes it feel premium without any paywall.** Rank is proof-of-rigor: Archivist standing, citation credit on ledger entries, and showcase of member detective work that caught a rumor early. Craft-showcase energy (INS-141) is spent on precision, not decoration. The ledger itself is the prestige object — being cited in it is the honor.

**What's deliberately left out at launch.** Crew registry and crew-vs-crew infrastructure (N9 deferred to listings-only minimum or nothing); build school and refereed debate (N7 minimal — substantive disagreement allowed but not staged); naval showcase programming (N8 minimal); ritual calendar beyond one containment space (C6 caution: no new formats until they can arrive with substance). Multilingual spaces beyond the static G7 blurb. Creator programming beyond prompt public mirroring of sourced material (INS-028/152).

**Needs and cards it responds to (primary).** N1 (INS-015/016/017/018/051/019 — testing/access truth with uncertainty marked); N2 (INS-001/004/005/007/012/014/021/022/025/029/112/116/117/118/121 — date credibility, never manufacture); N3 (INS-042/043/044/045/052/053/124 — oath verifiable, cosmetics-only red line, speculation never stated as fact); N11 (INS-004/014/023/027/116/117/118/119/120/124/125/127/140/141/150 — evidence-first ledger, leak literacy, visible feedback uptake); N12 (INS-013/047/049/054/122/123/128 — scam/IP safety, readable rules with appeals, containment not purges). Secondary: N4 (INS-030–041 — platform answers sourced-only with benchmarks marked unverified); N5 minimal (INS-070/080 — comparison threads refereed by evidence, cautionary promise-archive); N13 minimal containment (INS-003/105/106/108/112/126 — one salt space, grief-humor allowed there, serious spaces protected).

**Conflict calls (what makes it distinct).** C1: takes no ship-timing stance (fan-run, Gap G1). C2: hosts the EAC split via a single sourced-access record, resolves nothing. C5: milestones-only as the breaker; manifesto words not curated (comfort deferred to containment space). C6: maximal caution — almost no rituals; any new practice must arrive with receipts. C7: leanest containment — exactly one slowmode vent space from day one (demand wins over lean doctrine, but lean-ness preserved by keeping it to one). C3: defers mega-crew question entirely (no registry at launch). C10: strictest rumor rule — folk prices/benchmarks/roadmaps never restated, only rumor-tagged or omitted.

## Concept 2 — The Crucible

**Core identity hook.** "Bring your take, leave with proof." A refereed arena where rivalry, build identity, and acquisition literacy are contested out in the open — with referees, not pile-ons.

**Who this is for.** Rivalry-confident migrants and systems-hungry theorycrafters who want to argue fruit-vs-fruit, Haki-vs-fruit, and build-vs-tier-list without the discussion collapsing into insults or tier-list warfare (N5/N6/N7). Turns the corpus's biggest volumes (fruit topic 48.5K, fruit-comparison topic 35.4K) into the server's engine instead of its hazard.

**Category skeleton (7, high level).**
1. Entry + fan-run notice, rules, conduct code for debate (onboarding screen; code stresses evidence over volume).
2. Arena (structured debate space with referee norms; comparison threads live here and only here).
3. Build school (identity-first builds, playstyle explanations, migrant combat-culture explainer; tier-list warfare redirected into build threads).
4. Acquisition + trade norms (rarity/pity literacy with rumor-tags enforced; fair-trade norms as black-market undercut; no odds/numbers stated — G4).
5. Help desk (forum-type Q&A incl. platform and scam-check; device-experience answers welcomed, specs marked uncertain).
6. Community hearth (introductions, showcases, light waiting-humor; heckler-fatigue rule protects wounds, not chants — INS-107).
7. Containment + moderation transparency (salt space with slowmode from day one; referee calls recorded in the private mod-action log, with any public summary a Phase 4 opt-in).

**Operator note (one-operator viability).** No live bouts and no live refereeing at launch: arena threads are async, always under slowmode + AutoMod, and referee action is a best-effort queue the operator clears on 10–15-minute check-ins. If no referee is present, threads simply cool and wait — nothing breaks, no round stalls, no result is owed on a schedule. Referee roles are not staffed at launch; Proven members may flag evidence fouls via the report path, and formal Referee powers (if any) are a Phase 4/5 staffing question.

**Role ladder (high level).** Newcomer → Contender (participates in arena/build spaces in good standing) → Proven (earned via consistently substantive, sourced, respectful argument or build craft) → Referee (earned trust to call evidence fouls and cool threads) → Warden (operator + rare deputies). The climb is conduct + substance, never spend or tenure alone.

**What makes it feel premium without any paywall.** Earned combat-of-ideas status: bout invitations, featured build breakdowns, referee trust. Skill-over-fruit creed (INS-058) and build-by-identity pride (INS-061) are the prestige currency. Menace power-fantasy stays playful through referee norms (N7).

**What's deliberately left out at launch.** Sourced trust ledger beyond a minimal confirmed-vs-rumor reference (N11 minimal — depth deferred); crew registry beyond a simple finder (N9 minimal, no caps/registry institutions); naval/RP programming beyond showcase threads (N8/N9 minimal); Shindo-tenure programming beyond a continuation framing line (N10 minimal); any date or milestone programming beyond honest labeling (N2 minimal — discipline enforced, not curated).

**Needs and cards it responds to (primary).** N5 (INS-009/067/068/069/070/071/072/074/075/076/077/080/148 — rivalry hosted never joined, evidence over insults, migrant onboarding with no shame for returnees, kill-talk contained); N6 (INS-055/060/063/064 — rare-but-fair legibility, usable commons, pity transparency as model, wishlist numbers stay rumor, fair-trade norms); N7 (INS-056/058/059/061/062/065/066 — progression/build identity over tier lists, Conqueror fantasy kept playful, race-theorycraft welcomed, balance disagreement substantive); N13-partial (INS-107/110/113/115/121/126 — hype self-discipline via referee norms, evidence outranks volume). Secondary: N1/N2/N3 minimal (honest labels enforced in-arena, oath visible, no invented dates/numbers); N4 (INS-036/037/039/041 — platform-tagged help, custom-controls asks, no platform-shaming, device volunteers recognized); N12 (INS-039/062/077/110/139/122/123 — conduct expectations, slang literacy, light-touch legitimacy); N14-creator (INS-109/152/028 — creator help economy in English, prompt mirroring, credit frames).

**Conflict calls (what makes it distinct).** C1: no ship stance. C2: hosts via arena threads (both EAC voices get refereed rooms). C3: restrains mega-factions by design — finder only, no institutional registry at launch, solo lane explicitly protected (opposite of Harbor). C4: celebration wins on affect inside showcase threads, restraint wins on every scope claim (showcase joy freely, spec sheets sourced-or-uncertain). C5: bouts and build features are the breaker (neither manifestos nor external milestones — the arena's own events reset salt). C6: exactly one recurring ritual (an async refereed bout/showcase cycle with no fixed schedule) and only with sourced substance per INS-118's constraint. C7: containment-via-arena — salt is redirected into structured threads with slowmode rather than a free-form pit. C9: chant/reply-guy/single-video guardrails enforced by referees (791 is hype not a question; 255 treated as medium; creator phenomena routed to help hubs).

## Concept 3 — The Harbor

**Core identity hook.** "Find your crew before you set sail." A port town where crews, professions, factions, and ships are the identity system — and solo sailors are explicitly welcome at the same dock.

**Who this is for.** Social players and pre-launch organizers who need to find crews by language/playstyle without stale listings, pursue Marine/civilian/profession/RP identities beyond the pirate binary, and see their naval fantasy taken seriously (N8/N9). Answers solo fear (INS-092) structurally instead of verbally.

**Category skeleton (8, high level — the cap).**
1. Entry + fan-run notice, rules, report path (onboarding screen; solo-viability stated at entry so solo players don't bounce).
2. Docks (crew finding by playstyle/faction/intent with self-serve freshness — listings expire unless the author re-confirms, so no human chases stale posts; large pre-launch orgs recorded as institutions with conduct expectations, chartered in batches).
3. Factions + professions + RP (Marine-story lane, civilian path, profession onboarding, organized-RP coordination in English; witness-reputation stays fan-design and labeled so — INS-085).
4. Ships + ocean (ships-as-home identity, naval feedback taken seriously, showcase of ocean joy; scope claims sourced-or-uncertain per C4).
5. Quests + events (player-written quest concepts with credit — INS-103; sea-event and clan-war interest registered without inventing competitive structures — G6).
6. Help desk (forum-type Q&A: private-server and timed-drop answers state only what is sourced, mark the rest unknown; platform help with no contempt).
7. Community hearth (introductions, reunion boards for lost-friend groups — INS-091, showcases).
8. Containment + moderation transparency (one tavern-style salt space with slowmode from day one; private mod-action log; scam-watch notices).

**Operator note (one-operator viability).** Chartering and freshness cost no daily enforcement: listings self-expire, charter grants happen in batches on check-ins, and conduct expectations are enforced reactively via the report path only (no patrols, no compliance audits at launch). If the operator is away, the Docks simply accumulate pending charter requests — finding still works, grants wait.

**Role ladder (high level).** Drifter (new) → Deckhand (verified participant) → Crewmate (crew-affiliated, via chartered crews) *or* Solo Banner (explicit opt-in solo path with equal standing) → Quartermaster (earned: crew officers, RP/profession organizers, consistently helpful members) → Harbormaster (operator + rare deputies). Tenure acknowledged alongside, not above, contribution. Mega-crew officers earn status through conduct, not headcount.

**What makes it feel premium without any paywall.** Chartered-crew standing, ship showcases, profession/RP badges, and reunion honors. Ships as homes (INS-093), rank ladders as retention (INS-083), and large orgs as institutions (INS-090) do the premium work that money must never do. Solo Banner parity is the legitimating detail — the server respects the lone sailor as much as the fleet.

**What's deliberately left out at launch.** Deep trust ledger (N11 minimal — confirmed-vs-rumor reference only, no archival apparatus); refereed debate arena (N5 minimal — comparison threads allowed but not staged, evidence norm enforced without bouts); build school depth (N7 minimal — identity builds welcomed, no curriculum); waiting-ritual programming beyond one tavern space (N13 minimal); Shindo-tenure programming beyond continuation framing + reunion boards (N10 secondary).

**Needs and cards it responds to (primary).** N8 (INS-057/093/094/095/096/097/098/101/102/103/104 — living ocean, ships-as-home, moving-ship credibility, ocean-as-draw over fast travel, side quests with real rewards, sea beasts as endgame, competitive interest registered not invented, AAA comparisons restrained); N9 (INS-081/082/083/084/085/086/087/088/089/090/091/092 — finder by language/playstyle with freshness, faction/RP/profession lanes beyond binary, private-server/timed-drop straight answers with unknowns marked, cross-language recruiting acknowledged within English-only scope, large orgs as institutions, reunion vs grief both honored, solo viable); N10-partial (INS-091/143/145/146/002 — reunion boards, life-phase return doors, tenure as identity). Secondary: N5-migrant onboarding via crews (INS-009/068/071/072/074); N6 trade norms inside crew economy (INS-055/063 fair-trade); N7 identity builds inside crew roles (INS-061); N4 mixed-device culture free of contempt (INS-039/041); N12 scam/IP safety + readable rules with appeals (INS-013/047/049/123).

**Conflict calls (what makes it distinct).** C1: no ship stance. C2: hosts via FAQ-style sourced answers (debate not staged). C3: the opposite of Crucible — embraces large crews as launch institutions with charters, freshness rules, and conduct expectations, while protecting the solo lane structurally (Solo Banner parity + mega-faction restraint norm; Phase 4 load case is mega-crew domination per INS-122). C4: celebration-through-ships with strict scope restraint. C5: crew events and quest showcases are the breaker (community milestones, not manifestos). C6: rituals decentralized to crews (server runs almost none itself; crews may, with receipt norms). C7: tavern-model containment — one social salt space, not an arena or archive annex.

## Concept 4 — The Long Watch

**Core identity hook.** "The wait is the voyage." A lighthouse for the pre-launch years where waiting itself is given craft: salt has a room, patience has a record, return has a door, and no milestone is ever faked to keep the light on.

**Who this is for.** Long-haul waiters, aging-out veterans, and forget-until-launch drifters who need disappointment to get ritual rather than suppression — and proof rather than pep talks (N13/N10/N2). Designs for day-one emptiness: a nearly-empty Watch still feels warm because the structure expects few voices and honors each one.

**Category skeleton (7, high level).**
1. Entry + fan-run notice, rules, report path (onboarding screen; waiting norms stated up front: salt belongs in its room, serious spaces stay serious).
2. Watch deck (waiting together: grief-humor, year-memes, chants — contained here by design with slowmode; heckler-fatigue rule protects wounded members per INS-107).
3. Milestone board (shipped milestones only — never words-as-milestones; dateless drops honestly labeled; breaks salt liturgy per INS-112; doubles as the N11-lite trust record).
4. Hearth (introductions, tenure honors, reunion/return doors tied to real milestones only, Shindo-continuation framing — never replacement).
5. Ocean + help (naval joy showcased freely with scope restraint; forum-type Q&A for testing/platform/scam-check with sourced-only answers).
6. Community craft (patience manifestos curated as revival material — INS-111 — plus myth-writing and slang glossary — INS-110/113; creator frames credited — INS-152).
7. Moderation transparency (private mod-action log, readable rules with appeals, scam-watch notices; doom and hope both given rooms per INS-126).

**Role ladder (high level).** Castaway (new) → Watchkeeper (verified participant in good standing) → Beacon (earned: steady, kind, evidence-honest presence during salt peaks; helpers and device-experience sharers) → Elder (earned: long tenure + sustained steadiness; rare, never purchasable) → Keeper (operator + rare deputies). Time alone doesn't promote — steadiness during hard weeks does.

**What makes it feel premium without any paywall.** Tenure made visible (day-zero/2019/both-Shinobi-Lives honors per INS-146 without inventing a lineage system), ritual-keeper status, myth and manifesto curation with author credit, return honors. The most-endorsed YEAR-joke camaraderie (sentiment §4) and work-ethic praise reservoir (INS-140) are spent on belonging, not hype.

**What's deliberately left out at launch.** Deep ledger apparatus (N11-lite only — milestone board, no archival bureaucracy); refereed arena (N5 minimal — rivalry pride shareable, attack lines contained, no staged bouts); crew registry (N9 minimal — reunion boards and simple finder only, no charters/caps); build/acquisition depth (N6/N7 minimal — identity builds welcomed, no school or trade apparatus); multilingual spaces beyond the static G7 blurb.

**Needs and cards it responds to (primary).** N13 (INS-003/008/105/106/107/108/110/111/112/113/115/121/126 — salt/grief-humor/chants/hype containable without flooding or purges, shared evidence-over-volume norms, disappointment ritualized, doom + hope both roomed); N10 (INS-002/006/073/078/079/143/144/145/146/150/151/114/050 — Seas as continuation never replacement, tenure as identity, low-pressure return tied to real milestones, childhood infrastructure honored, joint-news-days spirit without promising them); N2 (INS-001/004/007/012/014/021/022/025/029/112/116/117/118/121 — date discipline, uncertainty and sources on every window, dateless drops honestly labeled); N11-lite (INS-004/014/112/116/140/141 — milestone board as trust ledger, work-ethic praise spent on patience, craft showcased). Secondary: N1/N3/N4 minimal (honest labels, oath visible, platform answers sourced-only); N5 pride-without-attacks (INS-068/069/074/148); N12 (INS-122/123/128 — light rivalry meaning vs domination fear, readable rules with appeals, boycott speech as protected criticism met with evidence); N8 joy (INS-097/101 — ocean/beast enthusiasm showcased, scope restrained).

**Conflict calls (what makes it distinct).** C1: no ship stance (both voices welcomed in the Watch, neither adopted). C2: hosts via milestone-board FAQs. C3: sidesteps — reunion boards + simple finder only, no institutional crew bets at launch (opposite of Harbor's embrace and Crucible's restraint-by-finder; Watch defers the question). C4: celebration-heavy on affect (ocean joy, myths, chants) with the strictest scope labeling (every showcase tagged confirmed-or-community-wish). C5: both-and — manifestos as triage (INS-111) *and* milestones as the breaker (INS-112), explicitly sequenced (words soothe for days, only shipped things reset the ledger). C6: most ritual-tolerant but gated — rituals allowed only with receipts and sources attached (INS-118 constrains every addition). C7: containment-as-identity — the Watch deck exists from day one as the server's core room, not an annex (lean-ness preserved by keeping everything else small).
