# Phase 2 — Master Synthesis (player needs, not solutions)

Date: 2026-10-06. Scope: one session, per `build/briefs/phase-2-synthesis.md`.
Inputs: all 12 shard digests (`build/digests/shard-S01-digest.md` … `shard-S12-digest.md`, covering INS-001–INS-152), `insights/insights.stats.md` top-10 by volume, `findings/comment-sentiment-findings.md` (weighted verdict, wins conflicts), `findings/discord-architecture-research.md` (supply-side doctrine only).
Output: this file. No server design — needs only. Design is Phase 3.

Method: needs are consolidated from card `discord_decision` intent, lifted to need/outcome level (no channels, roles, bots, rituals, or events proposed here — those are Phase 3). "Need:" lines describe the player-required outcome, not the structure that will deliver it. Ranking blends (a) `insights.stats.md` card volume scores, (b) sentiment topic/cluster volumes and §1–§2 weighted ranks, (c) breadth (row counts + cross-shard recurrence). Card volumes are counted sums, not estimates. Ranking judgment: where card-volume order and sentiment weighted ranks differ, the synthesis follows the sentiment weighted order as the ranking tiebreak (breadth still cited). AGENTS.md rule 3 (sentiment wins conflicts with other findings memos) is applied separately in Conflicts, not as a general volume override. Sentiment §5 is cited only for demand-side urgency signals, never for structures. Where no clean single volume exists, breadth (rows + recurrence) is cited instead.
