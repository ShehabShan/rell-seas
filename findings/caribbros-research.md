# RELL Seas Comment Culture: Research & Strategy Report

## A. Per-Video-Cluster Voice Analysis

* **QNA (Questions, No Answers):** Embodies chronic gaslighting and institutionalized confusion. The community enters seeking concrete dates and leaves with ontological crises, coining the defining moniker "Questions, No Answers." It is playful despair mixed with deep-seated trust issues toward the developers.
* **Anime (Ragebait-Forbearance):** Represents a psychological judo match. The community recognizes they are being trolled with an animated series instead of the game, yet surrenders entirely because the production value shatters expectations of what a Roblox-adjacent studio can achieve.
* **When is M3 (Movie 3 Detective Work):** Driven by forensic paranoia and disbelief. Every developer quote ("We’re 80% done") is parsed like ancient scripture, leading fans to genuinely question if the developers are high, trolling, or running a multi-year sociological experiment.
* **Comms (Breadcrumb Memes):** The voice of the terminal famine victim. Utilizing hyper-exaggerated nihilism ("2048," "trailers to the trailer"), this cluster treats marketing crumbs as subsistence rations while maintaining a dark, satirical gallows humor.
* **City Boys (Cinema-Beauty & Schizo-Numerology):** Splits into two distinct personas: absolute aesthetic reverence ("1 minute of absolute cinema") and unhinged QAnon-style frame math where random seconds and blades of grass are calculated to uncover hidden release dates.
* **Appreciate You (Gratitude & Scam Warnings):** The emotional baseline of the fanbase—raw, vulnerable, long-term loyalty (since 2021/2022) juxtaposed with frantic community policing against malicious Roblox copycat scams and fake download links.
* **Chant (Notification Trauma):** Pure PTSD. Every upload notification triggers collective cardiac arrest, followed by the immediate, grieving realization that it is *anything* except Movie 3, jokingly calculating how reading the update just delayed the game another 365 days.
* **Gear 3 (Hype & Scale):** Pure visceral adrenaline and economic dread. Fans shift instantly from mocking 5-second teaser clips to plotting endgame raids ("beating Kaido") and mocking their future empty wallets.
* **Shindo Coexistence (Nostalgia & COVID PTSD):** A bridge across eras. It blends deep-seated COVID-19 lockdown nostalgia with active Shindo Life players dealing with broken mechanics, proving the community's roots run deep in the CaribBros ecosystem.
