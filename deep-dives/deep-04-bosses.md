### C. Boss Mechanics Culture
To master RELL Seas, players must move beyond "stat-checking." The game utilizes a **Combat Flag System**:
*   **Parry/Dodge/Block Flags:** Every attack is coded with specific interaction requirements. Failure to identify the flag results in high-damage penalties.
*   **Star Difficulty:** A 1–5 scale indicating the mechanical complexity of the encounter.
*   **Drops:** Bosses are the primary source for high-tier weapons and crafting materials.
*   **Rebalance Caveat:** All listed damage values (e.g., Alucard’s 720 Slam) are wiki-listed as of 2026-07-07 and are highly likely to shift during the final balancing pass before launch.
