## D. Your Crew‑Academy Blueprint

> **Goal** – Offer a *4‑week structured program* that turns newcomers into battle‑ready crew members, culminating in a directory listing and a custom Jolly‑Roger reveal.

### 1. Week 1 – Manifest & Roles

| Day | Activity | Outcome |
|-----|----------|---------|
| 1 | **Kick‑off AMA** – Q&A with senior crew leaders. | Build trust. |
| 2 | **Manifest Release** – Publish crew manifesto (mission, values, expectations). | Sets culture. |
| 3 | **Role Definition** – Outline 5 core roles: Captain, Navigator, Quartermaster, Shipwright, Lookout. | Clarifies responsibilities. |
| 4 | **Recruitment Post** – Invite applicants via Discord. | Start roster. |
| 5 | **Role‑Specific Channels** – Create dedicated channels for each role. | Keeps discussions focused. |
| 6 | **Orientation Video** – Walkthrough of the crew system, bounty, and directory. | Visual learning. |
| 7 | **Open Discussion** – Feedback on manifesto and roles. | Iterate. |

**Roles & Responsibilities**

| Role | Core Duties |
|------|-------------|
| **Captain** | Overall strategy, final decision‑making. |
| **Navigator** | Map planning, route optimization. |
| **Quartermaster** | Resource allocation, inventory. |
| **Shipwright** | Ship upgrades, maintenance. |
| **Lookout** | Recon, enemy spotting. |

1. **Respect** – No harassment or hate speech.
