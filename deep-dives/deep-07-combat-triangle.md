|------|--------------|--------------------------|--------------------------------------|----------------------|
| **Armament R** | Grants invisible armor that can **bypass Logia‑type immunity**. | 6 cards | • Works on any weapon class.<br>• Can be combined with Observation T to anticipate enemy moves.<br>• When paired with Conqueror G it can **disable fruit powers** on hit. | Full‑body hue + coverage + placement across **4 independent components** (e.g., shoulders, torso, limbs, aura). | **Observation T** | “Feel” the presence of opponents, granting heightened awareness. | 6 cards | • Increases reaction windows for Flash‑Dash, Geppo and weapon timing.<br>• Synergises with Armament R to know *when* the armor will be most effective. | Same 4‑component system – often visualised as subtle glows or “sensor” lines. |
| **Conqueror G** | Overwhelming presence that can **disable fruit powers** on affected foes. | 5 cards | • Only a subset of players can unlock (randomised path).<br>• Works best when the opponent relies on a Devil‑Fruit; otherwise it functions as a massive knock‑back/area‑control tool. | Often rendered as a radiant aura that can be toggled on/off; colour can be distinct from Armament/Observation layers. |

### 1. Card Acquisition & Progression  
* **Collectible Haki Cards** are obtained through the **Spiritual tab** at a “character‑development point” (exact trigger undisclosed – **SPECULATION**).  
* **Randomised unlock paths**: No two players share the same sequence of card unlocks, meaning the meta will be highly personal.  
* **Remappable keys**: All Haki abilities can be bound to any key, allowing custom combos.  

### 2. Fruit‑Counter Matrix (Prose)  
* **Armament R** is the *only* Haki form that explicitly **bypasses Logia immunity** – a crucial tool against Logia‑type Devil‑Fruits.  
* **Conqueror G** can **disable fruit powers** on hit, making it a universal counter to *any* fruit (including Paramecia and Zoan) when the effect lands.  
* **Observation T** does **not** directly counter fruit abilities, but its awareness boost lets you **anticipate** fruit‑based attacks and dodge them with Flash‑Dash or Geppo.

> **How the layers multiply**  
> 1. **Weapon Class** (bond) → determines EXP growth & base damage profile.  
> 2. **God Fruit / Fruitless** → adds elemental/ability layer (e.g., fire, ice).  
> 3. **Haki Cards** (up to 6 + 5) → armor, perception, or crowd‑control overlay.  
> 4. **Fighting Style** → movement toolkit (Flash‑Dash, Geppo) and faction identity.  
> 5. **Poster** → augments style with extra combos or speed.  
> 6. **Race** → passive stat bonuses, possible style‑poster synergies.  
> 7. **Ship Role** (e.g., Cannon‑master, Navigator) → situational buffs.
